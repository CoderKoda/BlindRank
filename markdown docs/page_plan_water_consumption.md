# Page Plan: Water Consumption (`water.md`)

**Target File:** `water.html`  
**Role:** Grade 9 Web-Development Planning Document  
**Design Inspiration:** *Neal.fun* ("Printing Money" / visual accumulation & scale comparison)

---

## 1. Page Purpose & Core Mechanic
* **Purpose:** Reveal how everyday water use (showers, food production, household chores) multiplies across communities and nations.
* **Neal.fun Visual Mechanic:** **"Filling the Tank"**
  * Inspired by *Neal.fun*'s progressive visual counters, scrolling fills proportional blue water containers and stacks relatable everyday objects.
  * **Base Visual Unit:** $1\text{ standard bathtub} \approx 150\text{ liters}$ (or $1\text{ Olympic swimming pool} = 2,500,000\text{ liters}$ for City/Country/World tiers).

---

## 2. The 6-Tier Data Breakdown (Daily / Annual Estimates)
*Note: Includes direct household water use (or total water footprint—to be clearly labeled on the page).*
1. **Average Person:** $\approx 150\text{–}300\text{ liters/day}$ (Visual: 1–2 full bathtubs per day)
2. **Average Household:** $\approx 500\text{–}1,000\text{ liters/day}$ (Visual: ~4–6 bathtubs per day)
3. **Average Neighborhood (~1,000 people):** $\approx 200,000\text{ liters/day}$ (Visual: ~1,300 bathtubs)
4. **Average City (~500,000 people):** $\approx 100\text{ million liters/day}$ (Visual: 40 Olympic swimming pools daily)
5. **Average Country:** $\approx 10\text{–}300\text{ billion liters/day}$ (depending on country benchmark)
6. **Entire World:** $\approx 10+\text{ trillion liters/day}$ (including agriculture and industry)

---

## 3. Key Page Elements
* **Sticky Water-Level Gauge:** A blue progress bar or live counter at the top of the viewport showing total liters accumulated as the user scrolls.
* **Scale-Shift Callouts:** Clear visual transitions when switching from *Bathtubs* (Person/Household/Neighborhood) to *Olympic Swimming Pools* (City/Country/World) so the user understands the jump in magnitude.
* **Did You Know? Callout Cards:** Brief facts interspersed between tiers (e.g., *"It takes ~2,700 liters of water to make a single cotton t-shirt"*).

---

## 4. Acceptance Criteria
* Covers all 6 tiers (Person → World) with consistent timeframes (clearly labeled per day or per year).
* Uses intuitive visual icons (bathtubs/pools/droplets) to reflect scale in the spirit of *Neal.fun*.
* Smoothly handles huge numbers at the Country and World levels without breaking page layout.
