# Page Plan: Energy Consumption (`energy.md`)

**Target File:** `energy.html`  
**Role:** Grade 9 Web-Development Planning Document  
**Design Inspiration:** *Neal.fun* ("Printing Money" / visual accumulation & scale comparison)

---

## 1. Page Purpose & Core Mechanic
* **Purpose:** Show how much electrical and primary energy powers daily life—from charging a phone to running entire countries.
* **Neal.fun Visual Mechanic:** **"Powering the Grid"**
  * Uses high-contrast yellow/amber lightning bolts and glowing lightbulb icons against a clean dark-slate or off-white background.
  * **Base Visual Unit:** $10\text{ kWh}$ represented by 1 battery/lightning block (roughly enough to run a laptop for 150 hours or power a home's basic lighting for a day).

---

## 2. The 6-Tier Data Breakdown (Annual Estimates)
1. **Average Person:** $\approx 3,500\text{ kWh/year}$ (electricity) or $\approx 21,000\text{ kWh/year}$ (total primary energy)
2. **Average Household:** $\approx 10,500\text{ kWh/year}$ (Visual: ~1,050 energy blocks)
3. **Average Neighborhood (~1,000 people):** $\approx 3.5\text{ million kWh/year}$ ($3.5\text{ GWh}$)
4. **Average City (~500,000 people):** $\approx 1.75\text{ billion kWh/year}$ ($1.75\text{ TWh}$)
5. **Average Country:** $\approx 300\text{–}4,000\text{ TWh/year}$ (depending on benchmark nation)
6. **Entire World:** $\approx 29,000+\text{ TWh/year}$ (electricity) / $\approx 170,000+\text{ TWh/year}$ (total energy)

---

## 3. Key Page Elements
* **Sticky Wattage Ticker:** Live counter displaying $\text{kWh} \rightarrow \text{MWh} \rightarrow \text{GWh} \rightarrow \text{TWh}$ automatically converting units as the user scrolls deeper down the page.
* **Interactive Unit Explainer:** A small hover/click tooltip explaining what $1\text{ kWh}$ actually does in real life (e.g., *10 hours of watching TV*).
* **Visual Grid Compression:** Demonstrates *Neal.fun*'s signature compression—100 small battery icons merge into 1 big "Power Plant" block as you enter the City and Country sections.

---

## 4. Acceptance Criteria
* Accurately progresses across all 6 tiers (Person → Household → Neighborhood → City → Country → World).
* Clearly distinguishes between *electrical energy* and *total energy* so statistics are scientifically accurate for Grade 9 standards.
* Visual accumulation feels snappy, responsive, and easy to compare across tiers.
