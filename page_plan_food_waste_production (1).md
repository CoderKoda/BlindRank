# Page Plan: Food Waste Production (`food-waste.html`)

**Target File:** `food-waste.html`  
**Role:** Grade 9 Web-Development Planning Document  
**Design Inspiration:** *Neal.fun* ("Printing Money" / visual accumulation & scale comparison)

---

## 1. Page Purpose & Core Mechanic

* **Purpose:** Help users grasp the massive scale of edible food thrown away every year—from a single household kitchen to global food loss—and connect it to wasted resources and emissions.
* **Neal.fun Visual Mechanic:** **"Filling the Landfill"**
  * Uses recognizable everyday food icons (apples, loaves of bread, and full grocery bags) that stack up in neat grids as the user scrolls, then compress into shipping containers or garbage trucks at massive scales.
  * **Base Visual Unit:** $1\text{ kg of food waste}$ represented by 1 grocery bag / basket of food (roughly $\approx 2\text{–}3\text{ full meals}$), scaling up to $1\text{ garbage truck} \approx 10,000\text{ kg}$ ($10\text{ metric tons}$) for Neighborhood, City, Country, and World tiers.

---

## 2. The 6-Tier Data Breakdown (Annual Estimates)

*Note: Based on UNEP Food Waste Index Report & FAO global averages ($\approx 79\text{ kg}$ per person/year at the household level, and $\approx 130+\text{ kg}$ per person/year when including retail and food service).*

1. **Average Person:** $\approx 79\text{ kg/year}$ (Visual: 79 food baskets / $\approx 160\text{–}200\text{ meals}$ wasted annually)
2. **Average Household:** $\approx 240\text{–}315\text{ kg/year}$ (Visual: $\approx 275$ baskets, roughly the weight of an adult grizzly bear)
3. **Average Neighborhood ($\approx 1,000$ people):** $\approx 79,000\text{ kg/year}$ ($79\text{ metric tons}$ / $\approx 8$ full garbage trucks)
4. **Average City ($\approx 500,000$ people):** $\approx 39,500\text{ metric tons/year}$ ($\approx 3,950$ garbage trucks)
5. **Average Country:** $\approx 10\text{–}60\text{ million metric tons/year}$ (depending on country benchmark)
6. **Entire World:** $\approx 1.05\text{ billion metric tons/year}$ (household, retail, and food service combined — over $105\text{ million}$ garbage trucks)

---

## 3. Key Page Elements

* **Sticky Food Waste Counter:** Tracks total kilograms/tons of wasted food as the user scrolls, paired with a relatable real-world equivalent (e.g., *"Equivalent to X wasted meals"*).
* **6-Tier Visual Journey:** Clear section headers for Person, Household, Neighborhood, City, Country, and World with scroll-triggered icon accumulation and unit compression at larger scales.
* **Did You Know? Callout Cards:** Brief facts highlighting hidden impacts (e.g., *"If global food waste were a country, it would be the third-largest emitter of greenhouse gases"*).
* **Closing Summary & Action Card:** 3 practical tips to cut kitchen waste (meal planning, proper storage, eating leftovers) and navigation links to the other comparison pages.

---

## 4. Acceptance Criteria

* Presents all 6 required tiers (Person $\rightarrow$ World) with clear mass units ($\text{kg}$ and $\text{metric tons}$) and an annual timeframe.
* Uses *Neal.fun*-inspired repeatable visuals (food baskets/meals scaling up to garbage trucks) to make large numbers intuitive.
* Cites credible sources (e.g., *UNEP Food Waste Index Report*, *FAO*, *Our World in Data*) and maintains consistent navigation with the rest of the website.
