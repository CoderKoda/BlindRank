# Page Plan: Plastic Waste Production (`plastic-waste.html`)

**Target File:** `plastic-waste.html`  
**Role:** Grade 9 Web-Development Planning Document  
**Design Inspiration:** *Neal.fun* ("Printing Money" / visual accumulation & scale comparison)

---

## 1. Page Purpose & Core Mechanic

* **Purpose:** Illustrate the staggering volume of single-use and total plastic waste generated every year, scaling from a single person's trash bin to global plastic pollution.
* **Neal.fun Visual Mechanic:** **"Piling Up the Plastic"**
  * Uses recognizable plastic bottle and shopping bag icons that stack vertically and horizontally as the user scrolls, emphasizing how quickly non-biodegradable waste accumulates.
  * **Base Visual Unit:** $1\text{ kg of plastic waste}$ represented by 1 stack of plastic bottles/bags (roughly $\approx 40\text{ standard 500 mL plastic water bottles}$), scaling up to $1\text{ garbage truck} \approx 10,000\text{ kg}$ for City, Country, and World tiers.

---

## 2. The 6-Tier Data Breakdown (Annual Estimates)

*Note: Based on OECD Global Plastics Outlook & Our World in Data global/regional averages ($\approx 45\text{–}60\text{ kg}$ per person/year globally, higher in high-income nations).*

1. **Average Person:** $\approx 50\text{ kg/year}$ (Visual: 50 plastic stacks / $\approx 2,000$ plastic bottles)
2. **Average Household:** $\approx 150\text{–}200\text{ kg/year}$ (Visual: $\approx 175$ stacks, roughly the weight of an adult male lion)
3. **Average Neighborhood ($\approx 1,000$ people):** $\approx 50,000\text{ kg/year}$ ($50\text{ metric tons}$ / 5 full garbage trucks)
4. **Average City ($\approx 500,000$ people):** $\approx 25,000\text{ metric tons/year}$ (2,500 garbage trucks)
5. **Average Country:** $\approx 5\text{–}40\text{ million metric tons/year}$ (depending on country benchmark)
6. **Entire World:** $\approx 400+\text{ million metric tons/year}$ (over 40 million garbage trucks lined up)

---

## 3. Key Page Elements

* **Sticky Plastic Counter:** Tracks total kilograms/tons of plastic waste generated alongside a relatable real-world equivalent counter (e.g., *"Equivalent to X plastic water bottles"*).
* **6-Tier Visual Journey:** Clear section headers for Person, Household, Neighborhood, City, Country, and World with scroll-triggered icon grids and unit compression at larger scales.
* **Closing Summary & Action Card:** Highlights why plastic waste matters (microplastics, ocean pollution, centuries-long decomposition) and links back to the Homepage or About Us page to complete the site tour.

---

## 4. Acceptance Criteria

* Presents all 6 required tiers (Person $\rightarrow$ World) with clear mass units ($\text{kg}$ and $\text{metric tons}$).
* Uses *Neal.fun*-inspired repeatable visuals (plastic bottles/bags scaling up to garbage trucks) to make large numbers tangible.
* Cites credible sources (e.g., *OECD Global Plastics Outlook*, *Our World in Data*) and maintains consistent navigation with the other 5 pages.
