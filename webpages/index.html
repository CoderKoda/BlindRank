About This Project

Inspired by Neal Agarwal’s Printing Money, Consuming Earth translates abstract environmental statistics into real-time visual motion. Large numbers like "billions of kilowatt-hours" or "gigatons of carbon" are hard for the human brain to picture—seeing them scroll side-by-side makes the jump in scale immediately obvious.

Each page lets you compare everyday individual consumption against towns, major cities, nations, and the global population, and even enter your own hourly usage to see where you fit on the scale.
